Project 02

The Threshold

A liminal space experience exploring transition, uncertainty, and spatial boundaries.

►

Overview

Project Concept

The Threshold explores the feeling of moving between spaces. The project focuses on transition, uncertainty, and the psychological effect of being placed in an environment that feels familiar but incomplete. Instead of creating a clear destination, I wanted the space to feel like a temporary state between one place and another.

Design Inspiration

Liminal Space and Architecture

The design was influenced by liminal spaces, especially empty corridors, transitional rooms, and abandoned interiors. I was interested in how a digital environment can create tension without using characters or direct narrative. The space itself becomes the main source of mood.

The project also helped me think about thresholds as both physical and emotional boundaries. A doorway, corridor, or narrow opening can suggest movement, but it can also create hesitation. This became an important step in my understanding of environmental design.

Environment Design

Space, Light, and Atmosphere

In this work, I focused more on atmosphere than complex interaction. The environment uses simple architecture, controlled lighting, and empty space to create a sense of quiet tension. I wanted the player to feel that the space was not fully safe, even when nothing directly threatening was shown.

The visual design was kept minimal so that the player could focus on the feeling of the environment. Through this process, I began to understand how lighting, scale, and spatial rhythm can guide emotional response.

Production Process

Building the Transition

During production, I experimented with spatial layout, camera perspective, and the pacing of movement through the environment. I learned that even a simple space can feel meaningful if the player’s movement through it is carefully structured.

Compared with The Observer, this project moved beyond a single visual interaction. It helped me think more about how a whole environment can produce a psychological state.

Reflection

What I Learned

The Threshold was important because it changed how I thought about digital environments. I started to see space not only as something the player looks at, but as something that can shape mood and expectation. This project became a bridge between my early interest in observation and my later focus on behaviour control in COMPLIANCE.
