Project 01

The Observer

A minimalist interactive environment exploring observation, perception, and how small visual actions can change the structure of a digital space.

▶

Overview

Project Concept

The Observer is a minimalist interactive environment based on the relationship between looking and spatial change. The player enters a simple architectural space and discovers that certain walls can disappear when they observe the environment from the correct angle. The work focuses on perception rather than complex gameplay.

Process

Design and Production

My main design focus was to make observation feel like an active form of interaction. Instead of asking the player to collect items or solve a traditional puzzle, the project asks them to pay attention to the space itself. The disappearing wall became a simple way to show that the environment can respond to the player’s gaze.

The visual style is intentionally minimal. I used simple geometry, clean surfaces, and limited colour to avoid distracting the player. This helped me understand that an environment does not need many decorative elements to create interaction. The empty space, the wall placement, and the player’s viewing direction became the main design elements.

During production, I experimented with trigger areas, player camera direction, and object visibility. The technical system was simple, but it helped me learn how Unity can connect player perception with environmental change. This project also helped me become more confident with building small interactive scenes.

Reflection

What I Learned

Looking back, The Observer was an important starting point for my semester. At this stage, I was mainly interested in how a player reads a space visually. I learned that interaction does not always need to be obvious or action-heavy. A small change, such as a wall disappearing because of the player’s gaze, can already create a meaningful relationship between the player and the environment.
