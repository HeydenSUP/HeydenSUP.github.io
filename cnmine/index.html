02 · MEMORY

WHAT
CHANGED?
WHAT
REMAINED?

Today, the Gold Rush survives through museums, heritage sites and public memory. But remembering the past also means asking whose experiences are made visible, and whose are easily left out. Chinese communities became part of Victoria’s social and cultural landscape, while the history of exclusion remains part of the story.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT

FROM COLONIAL EXCLUSION
TO WHITE AUSTRALIA

1855

Victoria introduced the Chinese Immigration Act, restricting Chinese migration during the Gold Rush.

1901

After Federation, the Immigration Restriction Act 1901 formalised restrictive immigration at the national level and became a cornerstone of what became known as the White Australia policy.

1975

The Racial Discrimination Act removed the remaining legal basis for racial discrimination in Australian immigration policy.
