Project 03

COMPLIANCE

An interactive spatial experience about obedience, control, instruction, and behavioural conditioning.

COMPLIANCE Screenshot / Gameplay Recording

Overview

Concept

COMPLIANCE explores how interactive systems can condition player behaviour through instruction, restriction, repetition, and punishment. The player is placed inside a controlled environment and gradually learns to obey commands from an unseen system.

Environment

Spatial Design

The environment uses minimal geometry, repeated rooms, yellow fluorescent lighting, and Backrooms-inspired textures to create an uncomfortable but familiar institutional space.

Interaction

Command System

The interaction design is based around commands such as PROCEED, TURN RIGHT, LOOK UP, and DO NOT MOVE. These instructions shape the player’s movement, attention, and behaviour. When the player disobeys, the system responds through punishment, spatial correction, or removal.

Sound

Audio Design

The sound design combines physical environmental sounds, mechanical wall movements, warning tones, and shamisen string-pulling sounds. The contrast between realistic space sounds and sharp musical gestures helps create tension when the system intervenes.

Reflection

Final Reflection

This project represents a shift in my practice from building simple interactive functions toward designing emotional and psychological player experiences. I became more interested in how space, sound, camera, and restrictions can guide behaviour and communicate ideas.
